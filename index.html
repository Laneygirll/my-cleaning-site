Our Story

Founded by Acelino in 1994, Professional Cleaner has built a reputation for quality, integrity, and exceptional service. For over 30 years, our family has been dedicated to providing top-tier care. What started as a small venture in Bridgeport, CT, has grown into a trusted local business built on a foundation of hard work, reliability, and the simple joy of a truly clean home.

Today, the company proudly continues into its next generation under the leadership of Acelino's daughter and son-in-law, Andressa and Paulo. Together, they are committed to preserving the values that have defined Professional Cleaner for three decades while bringing a fresh vision for the future.

We don't just clean houses; we build lasting relationships with our clients, treating every home with the same care as our own. We are proud to be a part of this community and look forward to serving you.

Our Cleaning Services

Regular Cleaning

Weekly maintenance to keep your home fresh. Our complete residential cleaning covers every corner of your living spaces. We meticulously dust all surfaces, vacuum and mop floors, sanitize bathrooms, and clean kitchen counters, cabinets, and appliances. This service is fully customizable to fit your home's unique layout and your family's personal routine, ensuring a fresh and truly healthy living environment.

Deep Cleaning

Thorough scrub including baseboards and ovens. Deep cleaning goes far beyond the surface to tackle hidden dust and built-up grime. We detail high and low areas, including baseboards, window sills, door frames, light fixtures, and behind reachable appliances. Perfect for seasonal refreshing, preparing for special events, or restoring your home's original sparkle from top to bottom.

Move-In / Out

Spotless cleaning for transitions. Whether you are prepping a house to hand over keys or moving into a new home, we ensure the space looks spotless. This deep service includes scrubbing inside empty cabinets and drawers, detailed refrigerator and oven cleaning, and thorough sanitization of all rooms.

Commercial Cleaning

Tailored cleaning for offices and businesses. A clean workspace promotes productivity and leaves an exceptional impression on clients. Our commercial cleaning caters to offices, retail units, and shared business environments. We handle trash removal, desk and electronics dusting, floor care, and rigorous sanitization of breakrooms and common restrooms on a flexible schedule.
